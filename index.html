Questions

What score do I need for a certificate?

80% or higher. Anything lower shows your score immediately and lets you try again.

Whose name appears on the certificate?

Whatever you type in “Your Full Name for Certificate” before the quiz starts, so double-check your spelling.

What happens if I switch tabs?

A warning appears and our examiner is notified in real time. On the third switch, your quiz is submitted automatically.

How do I prove my certificate is real?

Share your certificate ID with anyone. They can enter it on the Verify page and inspect the exact certificate issued by us.

Can anyone create quizzes here?

No. 1M Quizverse is exclusively for 1 Million Ltd. assessments. Every quiz is authored and managed solely by our authorised administrators.

Who can see my results?

Only our authorised 1 Million Ltd. administrators can view student records, attempts, and certificates to run examinations and maintain official records.

Can I retake a quiz?

Yes. You can start any available quiz again from the Quizzes page whenever you are ready.
